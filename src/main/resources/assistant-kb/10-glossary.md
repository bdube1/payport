# Glossary

Plain-language definitions of terms used across Payport's products.

**Beneficiary** — a saved recipient (a phone number or biller account) in
Payport Pay, so you don't have to re-enter the details every time you
pay the same person or account.

**Biller** — a company or service you pay bills to through Payport
(ZESA, DStv, GOtv, TelOne, ZINWA, and others).

**Checkout Session** — a time-bound payment request created when a
merchant sends a customer to Payport's hosted payment page.

**Fiscalisation (ZIMRA)** — the process of formally reporting an invoice
(and, where relevant, a credit note against it) to the Zimbabwe Revenue
Authority as required by tax law. Payport Books supports this directly
as part of its invoicing flow.

**Fulfilment** — the actual delivery of what was paid for (the airtime
landing on a phone, a ZESA token being generated, a bill being marked
paid) — a distinct step that happens after the payment itself is
confirmed.

**KYC (Know Your Customer)** — the identity and business verification
documentation Payport reviews before approving a new merchant.

**Ops Portal** — Payport's internal tool for staff to run the platform —
transaction monitoring, merchant KYC review, reconciliation, and dispute
handling. Not a customer-facing product.

**Payment Link** — a shareable link a merchant generates for a specific
amount, which a customer can open and pay without any further setup.

**Reconciliation** — Payport actively checking with a payment provider
to confirm what really happened with a payment, used when the initial
response was delayed or unclear.

**Reference number** — the unique code assigned to every transaction
(format like `PP-MOB-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXX`) or merchant application, used to
look it up or discuss it with support.

**Settlement** — the periodic payout of a merchant's net transaction
proceeds (after fees) from Payport to the merchant's bank account.

**Vendor/Provider** — the mobile network operator, mobile money service,
or biller Payport integrates with to actually move money or deliver a
service (e.g. Econet, EcoCash, ZESA).

**Webhook** — an automatic notification Payport sends to a merchant's
own system when a transaction's status changes, so the merchant doesn't
have to keep checking manually.

**ZWG** — the Zimbabwe Gold currency; alongside USD, one of the two
currencies Payport's products support where a given service allows it.
