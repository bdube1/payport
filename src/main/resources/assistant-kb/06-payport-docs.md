# Payport Docs — Document Management

**Payport's own description:** *"Your documents, organised and secure.
Organise, manage and access business documents securely, from anywhere —
with a clear record of who did what."*

## Who it's for

Organisations that can't afford a lost or misfiled document. If important
paperwork lives across email threads, personal laptops, and physical
filing cabinets, Payport Docs gives an organisation one system of record —
with a clear trail of who accessed and changed what.

## What it does

- **Central storage** — every document in one secure place, not scattered
  across email and USB drives.
- **Access control** — decide exactly who can view, edit, or approve each
  document.
- **Version history** — see every change to a document and who made it.
- **Approval workflows** — route a document for sign-off without chasing
  people down manually.
- **Fast search** — find the document needed in seconds, not by scrolling
  through folders.
- **Secure sharing** — share documents externally without losing control
  of who has access.

## How a document moves through the system

Every document has a status that reflects exactly where it is in the
review process: **Draft → In Review → Approved** (or **Rejected**). A
rejected document can be revised and **Resubmitted** rather than starting
over from scratch, and a document can also be **Returned** to the
submitter for changes before a final decision. Documents no longer needed
in active workflow can be **Cancelled** or **Archived** without being
deleted outright.

This means a rejection isn't a dead end — the person who submitted a
document gets it back with the ability to fix and resubmit it, and the
full history of that back-and-forth (including every prior version) stays
attached to the document.

## Access and roles

Payport Docs uses role-based access, so an organisation can be specific
about who can merely view a document versus who can edit it or who has
approval authority over it — this is set per document/workflow, not an
all-or-nothing setting for the whole system.
