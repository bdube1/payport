# Legal, Privacy & Terms

This file summarises Payport's actual published Privacy Policy and Terms
of Service, in plain language. For the full legal text, direct people to
the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service pages on payport.africa — this
summary is for quickly answering common questions, not a substitute for
the published documents in a legal dispute.

## The legal entity

Payport's products are operated by **Payport Financial Services (Pvt)
Limited**, registered in Zimbabwe, headquartered at 232 Herbert Chitepo
Avenue, Harare.

## What Payport collects and why

- **Personal information** — name, email address, phone number, and
  payment details, collected when using Payport's services or website.
- **Usage information** — IP address, browser type, pages visited, and
  similar data from interacting with the website.
- **Cookies** — used to improve the browsing experience and understand
  website traffic; cookie preferences can be managed through browser
  settings.

This information is used to process payments, fulfil orders, provide
customer support, and — only with consent, and with an opt-out available
at any time — send promotional communications.

## Who Payport shares information with

Personal information may be shared with trusted third parties directly
involved in delivering the service — payment processors, banks, and
service providers — to facilitate payment processing and service
delivery. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, a
court order, or a valid legal request.

## Data retention and a customer's rights

Personal information is kept only as long as necessary for the purposes
it was collected for, or as required by law. Anyone can request:

- Access to their personal information
- Correction/updating of their information
- Deletion of their information (subject to legal and legitimate
  business obligations, e.g. transaction records that must be retained
  for a period under financial regulation)
- Information about how their data is being processed
- To object to certain processing of their information

Requests like these should go to support@payport.africa, not be handled
directly by Payport Assistant — this is a genuine data-rights request
that needs a human to action.

## What the Terms of Service cover

- Payport provides online payment solutions — payment gateway
  integration, secure transaction processing, and related services —
  alongside Payport School, Payport Books, and Payport Docs.
- Payment processing is available to individuals, companies, and
  merchants, covering mobile money wallets (EcoCash, OneMoney) and card
  payments (via ZimSwitch) — the terms describe this broadly; see the
  FAQ file for the distinction between what's described in the terms and
  what's confirmed actively working today for card networks specifically.
- Anyone with an account is responsible for keeping their login
  credentials confidential and for activity under their account.
- Payments are subject to processing fees and terms set by Payport's
  banking and payment-processing partners, in addition to Payport's own
  terms.
- Payport takes reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect data
  security but does not guarantee absolute security, and is not liable
  for unauthorised access it could not reasonably have prevented.
- All content, logos, and trademarks on Payport's website and products
  belong to Payport Financial Services (Pvt) Limited or its licensors.
- The Terms are governed by the laws of Zimbabwe, with disputes subject
  to the exclusive jurisdiction of Zimbabwean courts.
- Payport may update the Terms or Privacy Policy at any time; continued
  use of the service after an update counts as accepting the change.

## When to hand off to a human instead of answering directly

- Any formal data-deletion, data-access, or data-correction request
- Any dispute about liability, a specific financial loss, or a legal
  claim
- Any question asking for a legal interpretation of a specific clause
  in a way that amounts to legal advice

In all of these cases, the right answer is to explain what the policy
generally says (using this file), then route the person to
support@payport.africa rather than trying to resolve it directly.
