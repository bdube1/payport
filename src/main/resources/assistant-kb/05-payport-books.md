# Payport Books — Accounting & Financial Management

**Payport's own description:** *"Accounting that fits how your business
actually runs. Simplify accounting, inventory and financial management —
built for Zimbabwean businesses, in USD and ZWG."*

## Who it's for

Small and growing businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets — tracking
invoices in one file, stock in another, and bank statements in a third.
Payport Books brings it into one place a business owner, their
bookkeeper, and their accountant can all work from together.

## What it does

- **General ledger** — accurate books without needing an accounting
  degree to use the software.
- **Invoicing** — create and send invoices, track what has and hasn't
  been paid, and issue credit notes against them.
- **Inventory** — track stock levels alongside finances, not in a
  separate spreadsheet. Stock costing uses the weighted-average method.
- **Multi-currency** — manage USD and ZWG transactions side by side, with
  exchange rates captured directly by the business (not pulled
  automatically from a central bank feed), since manual control over the
  rate used is often what a Zimbabwean business actually needs.
- **Financial reports** — profit & loss, balance sheet, and cash flow,
  generated on demand.
- **Team access** — give a bookkeeper, an accountant, and management each
  the right level of access, rather than one shared login.

## Built for Zimbabwe specifically

Two features stand out as built directly for the Zimbabwean regulatory
and business environment:

- **ZIMRA fiscalisation** — invoices can be posted to ZIMRA (the Zimbabwe
  Revenue Authority) as part of the invoicing flow, including proper
  handling of credit notes issued against an already-fiscalised invoice
  (which also need to be reported to ZIMRA, not just adjusted internally).
- **Commission accounting** — for businesses that pay commission
  (matching commission expense to the revenue it relates to, rather than
  just expensing it whenever paid), plus a dedicated expense management
  workflow: an expense request goes through a two-person approval step
  before payment, and the person who submitted a request can't be the
  one who approves it.

## Banking

Payport Books supports an effectively unlimited number of bank accounts
and cashbooks as payment methods, since real businesses often operate
more than one account.
