# Payport — Company Overview

## Who Payport is

Payport is a Zimbabwean technology and financial solutions company,
headquartered at 232 Herbert Chitepo Avenue, Harare. The registered legal
entity is **Payport Financial Services (Pvt) Limited**. Payport started
as a payments gateway and has grown into a small family of four products
used by individuals, schools, and businesses across Zimbabwe.

**In Payport's own words:** *"We started with payments, and grew into a
small family of products used by individuals, schools and businesses
across the country."*

## The problem Payport set out to solve

Getting money moved in Zimbabwe — airtime, bills, transfers — involved too
much friction: queues, unreliable channels, and systems not built for how
people in Zimbabwe actually transact. Payport started as a payments
gateway to fix that specific problem, then grew into adjacent products
(accounting, document management, school administration) as the same
"it should just work" approach proved useful for schools and businesses
facing similar friction in their own operations.

## What Payport believes (company values)

- **Build the real thing** — "We'd rather ship something narrow that
  actually works than something broad that mostly does."
- **Local, not generic** — Products are built around how payments, schools,
  and businesses actually operate in Zimbabwe, not adapted from elsewhere.
- **Practical over flashy** — "Good software gets out of the way. We'd
  rather be useful every day than impressive once."

## The four products, at a glance

| Product | What it is | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Payport Pay** | A payments gateway — pay for airtime, data, and bills; merchants integrate it to accept payments | Individuals and businesses across Zimbabwe |
| **Payport Books** | Accounting, invoicing, inventory, and financial reporting | Small and growing businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets |
| **Payport Docs** | Secure document storage, version history, and approval workflows | Organisations that need one system of record for important paperwork |
| **Payport School** | Student records, fees, attendance, staff, and school operations management | School administrators, bursars, and teaching staff |

All four are built by the same team and follow the same underlying
principle: solve one specific, real operational problem well, for how
Zimbabwean organisations actually work.

## Where Payport operates

Based in Harare, building for Zimbabwe. Products serve individuals,
schools, and businesses across the country, with support available when
needed.
