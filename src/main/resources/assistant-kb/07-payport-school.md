# Payport School — School Management

**Payport's own description:** *"Everything a school needs to run
smoothly, in one system. Manage students, fees, teachers and day-to-day
operations — built for how Zimbabwean schools actually run."*

## Who it's for

School administrators, bursars, and teaching staff — whether running a
single school or several campuses. Payport School gives the
administration team, the bursar, and teachers a shared, accurate picture,
instead of spreadsheets scattered across different laptops.

## Core features

- **Student records** — enrolment, class allocation, guardians/parents,
  and full student history in one system.
- **Fee management** — track fee payments, balances, and statements per
  student or per class.
- **Staff & teachers** — staff records, class assignments, and timetables.
- **Attendance** — record and report attendance without paper registers,
  including device/QR-code-based check-in options.
- **Role-based access** — admins, teachers, and finance staff each see
  exactly what's relevant to their role.
- **Reporting** — generate the reports a school actually needs, on
  demand.

## Beyond the core — the fuller feature set

Payport School's scope goes well beyond basic student/fee records. Also
present in the system:

- **Academics** — subjects, timetabling, exams, exam schedules, results,
  and report cards.
- **Discipline** — tracking and recording disciplinary matters.
- **Library** — book/resource tracking and lending transactions.
- **Transport** — routes, vehicle/route assignment, and transport fees.
- **Hostel/boarding** — room allocation and boarding fees, for schools
  with boarding facilities.
- **Health records** — including vaccination tracking.
- **Clubs & sports** — membership, attendance, teams, and events.
- **School events** — planning, organisers, expenses, and feedback.
- **Financial literacy** — a learning module with quizzes, aimed at
  building students' financial literacy directly into the school system.
- **Assets & inventory** — school asset tracking, checkouts, and stock
  alerts.
- **Analytics & reporting** — dashboards, revenue forecasting, and
  predictive analytics on top of the school's own data.
- **Student wallet** — a cashless spending/wallet system for students
  within the school, with configurable limits.

## Getting started

A school administrator sets up the school's structure (academic year,
terms, classes) and staff accounts first; students, guardians, and
day-to-day records are then entered or imported. Role-based accounts mean
a bursar, a class teacher, and an administrator each log in and see the
tools relevant to them, not a single undifferentiated system.
