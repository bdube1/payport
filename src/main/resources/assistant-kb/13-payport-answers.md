# Payport answers: the questions people ask most

Each answer stands alone. Lines starting with "_Also:_" are other ways people ask the same question.
Payport serves customers on four channels that offer the same services: the website (app.payport.africa),
the Payport mobile app, USSD (dial *699#) and WhatsApp (+263 71 918 3557).

## Getting started

**Q: What is Payport?**
Payport is a Zimbabwean payment platform. You can buy airtime and data, pay bills (ZESA and other billers), pay shops and businesses, keep money in a Payport wallet, send money, and buy from online shops on Payport Store. You can use the website (app.payport.africa), the mobile app, USSD *699# or WhatsApp +263 71 918 3557.
_Also: what does payport do | tell me about payport | what can i do with payport | chii chinonzi payport_

**Q: How do I use Payport without internet?**
Dial *699# from any Econet, NetOne or Telecel line. The USSD menu lets you buy airtime and data, pay bills, pay a merchant, check a payment, register and, with a wallet, send money and cash out at an agent. No internet or smartphone is needed.
_Also: ussd code | what is the code | short code | dial code | which number do i dial | no data | no smartphone | feature phone | 699_

**Q: Can I use Payport on WhatsApp?**
Yes. Send "Hi" to +263 71 918 3557 on WhatsApp. You get the same menus as USSD: airtime, data, bills, paying merchants, checking a payment, your wallet and the stores. Reply with the number of the option you want; send MENU to start again.
_Also: whatsapp number | whatsapp banking | chat with payport | whatsapp menu_

**Q: Do I need an account to buy airtime or pay a bill?**
No. Anyone can buy airtime and data or pay a bill and pay with mobile money or a card, without an account. An account (your phone number and a 4-digit PIN) adds the Payport wallet, your history, saved numbers and faster repeat payments.
_Also: can i pay without registering | guest payment | must i register_

**Q: How do I register or open an account?**
In the mobile app or on app.payport.africa, choose Register and enter your phone number, your name and a 4-digit PIN. On USSD dial *699# and choose Register. Your account works on every channel with the same phone number and PIN.
_Also: sign up | create account | open account | how to join | register wallet | kunyoresa | ndingavhura sei account_

**Q: Where do I download the Payport app?**
The Payport app is for Android and iPhone. Until it is listed in your phone's app store, use the website app.payport.africa, which offers the same services, or USSD *699#.
_Also: download app | play store | app store | android app | iphone app | install payport_

**Q: Which networks does Payport support?**
Econet, NetOne and Telecel, for airtime, data and USSD *699#.

## Paying

**Q: How do I pay?**
Choose what to buy, enter the number or account, then choose how to pay: EcoCash, OneMoney, O'mari, InnBucks, a card (ZimSwitch, Visa or Mastercard) or your Payport wallet. For mobile money you approve the prompt on your phone with your mobile money PIN; the result shows straight away.
_Also: payment methods | ways to pay | how can i pay | what can i pay with | accepted payments | do you take ecocash | onemoney | innbucks | visa | mastercard | zimswitch_

**Q: How do I buy airtime?**
Choose Airtime, pick the network (Econet, NetOne or Telecel), enter the phone number and the amount, then pay. You can buy for your own number or anyone else's. On USSD: *699# then Buy airtime. On WhatsApp: send Hi and choose Buy airtime.
_Also: buy airtime | recharge | top up phone | airtime for my mother | send airtime | kutenga airtime | credit_

**Q: How do I buy data bundles?**
Choose Data, pick the network and the bundle, enter the phone number, then pay. The bundles shown are the ones the network offers today, with their price before you pay.
_Also: data bundle | buy data | internet bundle | whatsapp bundle | social bundle | mb | gb | data package_

**Q: How do I buy ZESA electricity tokens?**
Choose Pay a bill, then ZESA. Enter your meter number; the name on the meter is shown so you can check it before paying. Enter the amount and pay. Your token is shown on screen and sent by SMS. Enter the tokens into your meter in the order given.
_Also: zesa | electricity | prepaid electricity | token | meter number | buy units | magetsi | power_

**Q: My ZESA token is not working.**
Enter every digit of the token exactly as sent, and if you got more than one token, enter them in the order given. If the meter still rejects it, contact Payport on WhatsApp or by phone, +263 71 918 3557, with the payment reference.
_Also: token not working | token rejected | meter rejected token | invalid token | token not accepted | key change token_

**Q: Which bills can I pay?**
ZESA electricity and other billers such as Nyaradzo, Moonlight and ZOL, plus others listed under Pay a bill. The list in the app, on the website, on USSD and on WhatsApp is always the current one.
_Also: billers | bills | pay bills | nyaradzo | moonlight | zol | funeral policy | internet bill | dstv_

**Q: How do I pay a shop or business?**
Choose Pay a merchant and enter the merchant's Payport code, or scan its QR code in the app, then pay. You can also pay with a payment link the business sends you.
_Also: pay merchant | pay shop | merchant code | till number | scan qr | qr code | pay a business | payment link_

**Q: Are there charges?**
Any charge for a payment is shown before you confirm, together with the total. You never pay a charge you were not shown first.
_Also: fees | charges | how much does it cost | transaction fee | commission | is it free_

**Q: What are the limits?**
Limits depend on the service and, for the wallet, on your verification level: the more your identity is verified, the higher your limits. If a payment is over a limit, you are told before any money is taken.
_Also: limit | maximum | minimum | daily limit | how much can i send | transaction limit_

**Q: Which currencies can I use?**
USD and ZWG, depending on the service. You choose the currency before paying, and the wallet keeps one balance per currency.
_Also: currency | usd | zwg | zig | rtgs | bond | dollars_

## Results and problems

**Q: How do I know if my payment was successful?**
After you approve the payment on your phone, the app, the website and WhatsApp show the result: successful, or failed with the reason. On USSD the result comes by SMS. A successful purchase also comes by SMS, with any token or voucher. You can check any payment with its reference under Check a payment.
_Also: was my payment successful | did it go through | was it successful | result_

**Q: My payment failed. Was money taken?**
When a payment fails, no money is taken. The reason (for example insufficient funds or a wrong PIN) is shown. You can try again with the same or another payment method.
_Also: payment failed | declined | not successful | insufficient funds | wrong pin | failed transaction | it says failed | yakaramba_

**Q: Money was deducted but I did not get my airtime, data or token.**
If you paid and the purchase failed, Payport refunds you; you get an SMS saying it failed and that your payment will be refunded. If you have not had a result, send the payment reference here and I will check it, or check it under Check a payment. Do not pay again. If you still need help, WhatsApp or call +263 71 918 3557 with the reference.
_Also: money deducted | deducted no airtime | debited but not received | paid but nothing | did not receive token | paid but did not receive | paid for zesa but no token | paid but no data | no airtime | charged twice | mari yangu yabviswa | mari yabuda_

**Q: I did not get the prompt on my phone.**
Check that the number you paid from is right and has network signal, then try again. For EcoCash and OneMoney the prompt appears on the paying phone; for O'mari you enter the code sent by SMS; for InnBucks you approve the code in the InnBucks app. If no prompt comes, no money is taken.
_Also: no prompt | ecocash not working | mobile money not working | no ussd prompt | didn't get pin request | prompt not showing | no popup_

**Q: I approved the payment late. What happens?**
Each payment must be approved within its time window. If the window closes, the payment is cancelled and shown as failed. If money still left your account after that, it is refunded automatically: a late payment is never used.
_Also: timed out | timeout | approved late | took too long | expired payment_

**Q: How do I check a payment?**
Send me the payment reference (it starts with PP-) and I will tell you its status. You can also use Check a payment on USSD *699# or WhatsApp, or Track on the website and in the app.
_Also: check payment | track payment | trace transaction | reference number | where is my payment | status of my transaction_

**Q: How do refunds work?**
A refund goes back to where the money came from. You get an SMS when it is done. Refunds of store orders are made by the store through Payport.
_Also: refund | money back | reverse payment | reversal | when will i get my money back | how long does a refund take_

**Q: I sent airtime or paid to the wrong number.**
Contact Payport at once on WhatsApp or by phone, +263 71 918 3557, with the payment reference. Airtime already delivered to another number usually cannot be taken back, but the team will check what can be done.
_Also: wrong number | wrong account | mistake | sent to wrong person | wrong meter_

**Q: How do I make a complaint?**
WhatsApp or call +263 71 918 3557, or email info@payport.africa, with your payment reference. You get a complaint reference by SMS, and complaints are handled within set deadlines.
_Also: complain | complaint | report a problem | dispute | i am not happy | escalate_

## The Payport wallet

**Q: What is the Payport wallet?**
A wallet that keeps your money with Payport, protected by your 4-digit PIN. You can pay for airtime, data, bills, merchants and store orders from it, send money to other Payport wallets, and cash out at Payport agents. You have one balance per currency (USD and ZWG).
_Also: wallet | payport wallet | e-wallet | account balance | what is a wallet_

**Q: How do I top up my wallet?**
In the app or on the website open Wallet and choose Top up, then pay from mobile money or a card. You can also hand cash to a Payport agent. On USSD: *699#, My wallet, Top up.
_Also: top up wallet | add money | deposit | load wallet | fund wallet | cash in | kuisa mari_

**Q: How do I send money to someone?**
Choose Send money, enter their phone or wallet number and the amount, check the name shown, and confirm with your PIN. The money arrives straight away in their Payport wallet.
_Also: send money | transfer | p2p | send to another wallet | kutumira mari_

**Q: How do I cash out?**
Visit a Payport agent and choose Cash out at an agent (in the app, on the website or on USSD *699#). Enter the agent's code and the amount and confirm with your PIN; the agent gives you the cash.
_Also: cash out | withdraw | withdrawal | get cash | agent | kubvisa mari_

**Q: How do I check my balance?**
Your balances are on the app's home screen and under Wallet on the website. On USSD: *699#, My wallet, Balances.
_Also: balance | check balance | how much do i have | mari yasara_

**Q: How do I get a wallet statement?**
Open History in the app, or Wallet on the website, to see every wallet transaction; statements can be downloaded. On USSD: *699#, My wallet, Statement.
_Also: statement | history | transactions list | download statement_

**Q: How do I convert between USD and ZWG?**
Open your wallet and choose Convert. The rate and the amount you receive are shown before you confirm.
_Also: convert | exchange | exchange rate | usd to zwg | zwg to usd | forex | rate_

**Q: How do I raise my wallet limits?**
Limits rise as your identity is verified. Visit a Payport agent or contact Payport with your ID to be verified to a higher level.

**Q: My wallet is frozen or blocked.**
A wallet can be frozen to protect you or for a compliance check. Contact Payport on +263 71 918 3557 or info@payport.africa; only Payport's compliance team can unfreeze it.

## PIN and security

**Q: I forgot my PIN.**
Contact Payport on WhatsApp or by phone, +263 71 918 3557, or email info@payport.africa, to have it reset. After the reset you choose a new PIN when you next sign in.
_Also: forgot pin | reset pin | lost pin | forgot password | cannot remember pin_

**Q: How do I change my PIN?**
In the app or on the website open your profile and choose Change PIN. On USSD: *699#, More, Change PIN. After a change you sign in again with the new PIN.
_Also: change pin | new pin | update pin_

**Q: My account is locked.**
After several wrong PINs the account locks for a while to protect you. Wait and try again, or contact Payport on +263 71 918 3557 to have it unlocked.
_Also: locked out | account locked | too many attempts_

**Q: Is Payport safe?**
Yes. Payments are protected by your PIN and your mobile money PIN, all traffic is encrypted, PINs are never stored in readable form, and every transaction is recorded in Payport's ledger. Payport never asks for your PIN by phone, SMS or WhatsApp.
_Also: safe | secure | security | can i trust | is it legit | is this real | scam | fraud_

**Q: Someone asked for my PIN or a code.**
Never share your PIN or any code. Payport staff never ask for them. If you shared it, change your PIN at once and contact Payport on +263 71 918 3557.
_Also: someone asked for my pin | someone called asking for my pin | wants my pin | phishing | fake payport | scammer | suspicious message | otp scam_

## Payport Store

**Q: What is Payport Store?**
Payport Store (store.payport.africa) is where Payport businesses sell online. Browse stores by type, buy, choose pickup or delivery, and pay with EcoCash, OneMoney, O'mari, InnBucks, a card or your Payport wallet. Some stores also accept pay on delivery. The stores are also in the app, on WhatsApp and on USSD (Shop).
_Also: store | shop online | online shopping | marketplace | payport store | buy goods | stores near me | pay on delivery | cash on delivery_

**Q: How do I contact a seller?**
Open the store and choose Contact seller. You need to be signed in. Every conversation is kept on your Messages page, and the bell shows new replies.
_Also: contact seller | chat with seller | message the shop | ask the seller_

**Q: How do I track my store order?**
Open My orders in the store or in the app. The order shows whether it is paid, ready to collect, on its way or done.
_Also: my order | order status | track order | where is my order | delivery status_

**Q: My store order did not arrive.**
Contact the seller with Contact seller first. If it is not resolved, use Report this store, or contact Payport on +263 71 918 3557 with your order reference.
_Also: order not delivered | did not receive order | seller not responding | scam store | report a store | report a scam store | report this store_

**Q: How do I rate a store or review a product?**
After buying, open the store or the product and choose your stars; you can also write a review. Only buyers can rate and review.
_Also: rate | rating | review | stars | feedback_

**Q: How do I open a store?**
Register as a Payport merchant at merchant.payport.africa. Once approved, open the Store menu in the merchant portal to set up your store, add products with up to 5 pictures each, and start selling.

## Businesses (merchants)

**Q: How do I accept payments for my business?**
Register at merchant.payport.africa. After approval you can take payments by Payport code, QR code, payment links, POS and online checkout, see your sales and balances, and get paid to your bank account.
_Also: merchant | accept payments | business account | become a merchant | register business | collect payments_

**Q: What do I need to register as a merchant?**
Your business details, registration documents, the owner's ID and the bank account for payouts. Payport checks the application before approving it.
_Also: merchant requirements | documents needed | kyb | merchant onboarding_

**Q: When do merchants get paid?**
Money you collect is paid to your bank account in settlement batches. Your balances and payouts are shown in the merchant portal.
_Also: settlement | payout | when will i receive my money | merchant payout | bank transfer_

**Q: Can I integrate Payport into my website or app?**
Yes. In the merchant portal create API keys and use Payport's hosted checkout or API, with webhooks for payment results. Test keys work in sandbox first.
_Also: api | integration | developer | webhook | checkout api | plugin | ecommerce integration | sandbox_

**Q: How do I become a Payport agent?**
Contact Payport on +263 71 918 3557 or info@payport.africa. Agents serve customers with cash-in and cash-out, have their own portal for float and commission, and earn commission on each transaction.
